  5. ⭐ RATE CONDITION (manual)
     User assesses:
     - Overall condition: Good
     - Restoration: None
     - Authenticity: Probably authentic
     - Completeness: Complete
     - Rarity: Uncommon
     
  6. 📊 VIEW RESULTS
     App calculates estimate:
     €150 (median) × 1.0 (condition) × 1.0 (rarity) = €150
     Shows disclaimers and attribution

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ PRICE CALCULATION FORMULA                                                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  estimated_price = user_median_price × condition_factor × rarity_factor

  Where:
    condition_factor = overall × restoration × authenticity × completeness
    rarity_factor = user's rarity assessment

  Example:
    User median: €150
    Condition: Good (1.0) × No restoration (1.0) × Probably authentic (0.95) 
               × Complete (1.0) = 0.95
    Rarity: Uncommon (1.0)
    Result: €150 × 0.95 × 1.0 = €142.50
